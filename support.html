Support

DeductBook is in beta. Questions, problems and requests are welcome.

Contact

During the beta, the fastest way to reach us is to open an issue on GitHub. We answer within a couple of days.

Before you write

  • Lost records on a device? Device-only data lives in that browser. If you have a backup file, use Backup & data to restore it. If not, we can't recover it.
  • Sign-in link didn't arrive? Check spam, then request another. Links expire after a short time.
  • Invitation link says it was sent to a different address? Sign in with the exact email that was invited, or ask the household owner to send a new invitation to the address you use.
  • Something looks wrong in a total? Open the entry: the deduction check on the right explains every reduction and every missing record.

Deleting a household account

Email from the address you sign in with and say which household to delete. We remove its entries and receipt files and confirm by reply.
